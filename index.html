Design tips
Quick tips to improve your dress designing skills and create professional results.
Colour pairing
Pair warm fabrics like silk and brocade with jewel tones — deep emerald, royal blue, or burgundy. Lighter cotton works great with pastels and earthy neutrals.
Fabric & silhouette
Flowy fabrics like chiffon and georgette suit A-line and maxi styles. Structured fabrics like denim and brocade work best with fitted or tailored cuts.
Beginner tip
Always wash and iron your fabric before cutting. Fabric can shrink after the first wash, which may ruin your measurements and the final fit of the dress.
